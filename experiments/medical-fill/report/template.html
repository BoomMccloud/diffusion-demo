Synthetic Record Variety

At the same temperature, the dLLM writes more varied fictional patient records than Qwen3.6-35B-A3B. Raise Qwen's temperature to 1.2 and it matches, while the dLLM breaks down above 0.8. What the dLLM keeps is speed: it reaches the same number of distinct diagnoses in about a third of the time.

Distinct valid diagnoses over time

50 requests per model at 4 concurrent. A record counts only if it passes every check, and only when its diagnosis is new. Qwen runs through OpenRouter, so its time includes network and queueing.

What the runs show

  • Same temperatureAt 0.8 the dLLM produced 31 distinct diagnoses and 22 names from 43 valid records. Qwen produced 21 and 9 from 46, and named 28 of them 张伟.
  • Matched validityQwen at 1.2 reached 31 diagnoses and 24 names at 84% valid. The dLLM at 0.8 reached 31 and 22 at 86% valid, so the variety gap closes.
  • High temperatureThe dLLM fell to 60% valid at 1.0 and 2% at 1.2, where it wrote multilingual noise until the 512-token cap. Qwen stayed at 84% or above.
  • SpeedA batch of 50 took the dLLM 8 to 9 seconds at 0.6 and 0.8, and Qwen 21 to 26 seconds at every temperature.
  • Safety checkBoth models sometimes prescribed a drug in the same class as a listed allergy, such as 阿莫西林 for a 青霉素 allergy. The dLLM did it 3 to 6 times per 50 at 0.6 to 1.0, Qwen 1 to 4 times.
