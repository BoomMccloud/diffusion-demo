# YouTube KOL contact sheet

One row per creator for Clover's launch on Oct 28: how to reach them, what they've made that's relevant to us, where outreach stands, and the message to send. This replaces `youtube_kol_outreach.md`, `youtube_kol_messages.md` and `youtube_kol_send_sheet.md`. Background on why these creators: `youtube_launch_study.md`.

## How to use it

- **Route** says where to send. X means DM. Email means the address in the Email / contact column, with the subject given. Comment means a comment on their latest video. LinkedIn is only for IBM Technology (Martin Keen).
- **About email** means YouTube hides the address behind sign-in and a captcha. Open the channel's About page and click "View email address".
- **Status:** update it as you go (Sent with the date, Replied, Declined, Posted).
- Messages follow Jason's email: Clover, up to 700 tokens/second, early-access preview, no strings attached. Sent rows keep the message that went out.
- Speed: our 2026-10-16 probe measured about 700 tok/s on code and 270 to 310 on prose, one request at a time. Expect timed tests on chat to come in well under 700.
- Mercury 3 launched about 2026-10-19 with 1,000+ tok/s claims and 100M free tokens, and several of these creators covered it. Open weights are what Clover has that Mercury doesn't.
- Handles and contacts come from each channel's About links or the creator's own site, checked 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-24. "Recent relevant work" view counts are as YouTube showed them on 2026-10-24.
